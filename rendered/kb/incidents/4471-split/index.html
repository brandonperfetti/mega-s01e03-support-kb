The three problems

parser: dates read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 2 December comes out as 12 February. It reaches us by app, email and form, from every tenant. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. A day-first tenant’s people type what the hint says, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way. The symptom is the same swap, but the bug is in the form, not the parser. It comes only through the form, only from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to print the tenant’s order.

one-day-move: dates shift by one day

These reports describe a date that moved by a single day, not a swapped day and month: Tuesday 12 May showed as Monday 11 May; the same meeting landed on Tuesday for a client in Lisbon and on Monday for a client in Toronto. A day–month swap cannot do that. The desk read them as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). They stay counted under parser, where they are filed by tenant, and have a ticket of their own. The cause is not known yet; a time-zone conversion is the first thing to check.

Why it became one ticket

  • We merge on subject (decision 0001). All three problems come in with the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so the subject cannot tell them apart. The cause depends on the tenant, its booking form and the channel, which causes.ts already knows and the dedupe explorer shows.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. That guess disagrees with the problem for the reports counted under the table.
  • A month swap and a one-day move look alike in a subject line. Only the body tells them apart.

What the desk does now

  1. Keep #4471 for parser only. Merge a new report there only when its tenant writes the day first and it did not come through booking-v3’s form.
  2. Merge form reports from booking-v3 tenants into the hint ticket.
  3. Read the body before merging. A date that moved by one day goes to the one-day-move ticket, and its id goes into desk-notes.json; this table picks it up on the next build.
  4. Replace decision 0001 with a new record that dedupes by cause, not by subject.
